`/readyz` reports "warming" until the catalog is loaded and the retrieval index
is built. That is expected on a cold start, and the startup probe allows for it.

Before you point an agent at this, confirm the three things that decide whether
answers are right, not just governed:

  1. The catalog is the current one.
     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deploy/{{ include "querygate.fullname" . }} | grep "catalog ready"
     Watch querygate_catalog_models and querygate_catalog_synced_timestamp_seconds:
     a catalog that silently shrinks degrades every answer before anything errors.

  2. The models and metrics are documented. Retrieval and metric compilation can
     only be as good as the descriptions your team wrote; an undocumented mart
     produces confident, wrong answers.
{{- if .Values.config.certifiedOnlyRoles }}

  3. Certified-only roles are set to: {{ .Values.config.certifiedOnlyRoles }}
     Those principals can read certified metrics and nothing else. Make sure the
     metrics they need actually carry meta.certified in dbt, or they will be
     refused everything.
{{- else }}

  3. config.certifiedOnlyRoles is empty: every caller may write ad-hoc SQL, and
     no answer is marked as an agreed figure. Set it before exposing this to an
     audience that will quote the numbers as official.
{{- end }}
